FAQ

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Add your income goal, taxes, business expenses and a profit buffer to get the revenue you need. Then divide by the hours you can actually bill: working weeks × weekly hours × billable share. Most freelancers bill only 50–70% of the hours they work.

What's a good billable percentage?

Sales, admin, invoicing, learning and emails take up a lot of time. 60–70% is realistic for established freelancers. 40–50% is common in your first year.

Should I charge hourly or per project?

Use your hourly rate as the floor, then quote per project with a scope buffer. Fixed prices reward you for getting faster, and clients like knowing the cost up front.
